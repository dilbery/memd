Your memory, explained

What is here?

Memories saved by your {{ org_name() }} tools to your personal memd store. This is not a transcript of every conversation.

What can I change?

Edit a memory's title, content, summary and tags. Its identity and source history stay attached. If another tool changes it while you are editing, memd asks you to reload before saving.

What does retract mean?

A retracted memory is excluded from future recall and agent reads. It stays in your Retracted list and in history. Retraction is not permanent erasure, and it cannot remove information already copied into conversations.

Who can see this page?

Only your signed-in account can browse your store here. {{ user_group() }} membership is required. Token administration has a separate administrator login.
