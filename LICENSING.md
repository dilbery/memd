company, but if you make money from memd itself, get a commercial license so

The [LICENSE](LICENSE) file is the authoritative text. This page only
summarises it.

## Free to use

No permission is needed for:

- personal use, hobby projects, study, research and experiments;
- charities, schools and universities, public research organisations, public
  health and safety organisations, environmental organisations and government
  bodies;
- any organisation, including companies, running memd for its own internal
  operations, and changing it for that purpose.

## Needs a commercial license

Making money from memd itself needs a commercial license, including:

- selling or charging for memd, or for a copy or changed version of it;
- offering memd, or a product or service built on it, to anyone outside your
  organisation, including as a hosted or managed service;
- installing, running or customising memd for clients as part of paid work.

Commercial terms are kept simple and fair: a flat annual fee, a per-seat fee
or a share of the revenue memd helps earn. To ask about a license, open an issue titled
"Commercial license" on this repository, without any private details, and the
licensor will reply there or arrange a private channel.
